Three things break today

💻

Your laptop must stay on

Every watch is a script on your own machine. Close the lid, watching stops.

🔤

Alerts only match keywords

Google Alerts / IFTTT can't answer "is this bad news?" — meaning needs an LLM.

🤫

Watchers die silently

One cron job breaks, everything shares its fate. (My own crawler was dead 18 days before I noticed.)

Why these sponsors, not a VPS

📦

Daytona — a computer per watch

Born in ~2 s from one sentence, stateful while it lives, gone on /stop. 100 watches = 100 boxes that never touch each other. The bot itself runs there too — laptop closed.

🧠

Nosana — meaning, cheaply

"Negative news?" is a judgment, not a keyword. Running it on a Nosana GPU makes 24/7 judging affordable; without it this is Google Alerts.
